# The Platform Plan

*Part of our guide to [running an online community you actually own](/articles/how-to-run-an-online-community).*

The **Platform plan** is for organizations whose community is too important to run on a generic app: membership networks, associations and institutions that need their own app, their own data and a team behind both. It starts at **$6,000/mo**.

## Branded or Platform?

Flotilla's hosted plans ([Free, Basic, Growth and Branded](/pricing)) host a space inside Flotilla. **Branded**, from $300/mo, puts that space under your own name in the app stores, with your icon, colors and domain.

**Platform** is a different product. It includes everything in Branded and adds the engineering to make Flotilla fit your organization.

## What's included

- **Your own app.** Flotilla under your name on iOS, Android and the web. Members never see our brand.
- **Custom development.** Features built for how your organization works: chapter structures, member tiers, onboarding, reporting, whatever the off-the-shelf app doesn't do.
- **Integrations.** Your member database, single sign-on, payments and the rest of your stack, through full API access and automations with [n8n](https://n8n.io).
- **A say in the roadmap.** Platform customers get a seat at the table when we decide what to build next.
- **Dedicated hosting.** Servers, upgrades, scaling and backups are our job.
- **Dedicated support.** A direct line to the people who build Flotilla.
- **Migration.** We move your members, history and directory from your current platform, so no one starts from zero.

## Built for your developers

Platform also suits teams that want to build on top of their community, so it comes with hands-on developer support: direct access to our team, SDKs for your own features and integrations, documentation covering the APIs and integration points, and agent skill files so AI coding assistants can work directly with the platform.

## Why it costs what it does

Flotilla is open source, and hosting a community costs a few dollars a month. The Platform fee buys engineering, integration work and attention. There are no per-seat fees, no transaction fees, and your data is exportable at any time.
